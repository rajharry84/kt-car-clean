About Us

Who we are

Tej & Keerat

We are cousins, Tej and Keerat, and we are both 14 years old. We want to start our own car washing business because we enjoy working together and would like to earn some of our own money. We have noticed that many people in our neighbourhood are busy and don't always have time to wash their cars, so we thought this would be a good idea. We will work on weekends and after school so it doesn't affect our schoolwork. This business will also help us contribute towards our tuitions and support our education. We believe this will help us learn responsibility, teamwork, and confidence, and we will do our best to keep our customers happy.
